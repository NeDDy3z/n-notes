Base2Tone-nvim colour pair presets

Morning, Evening, Sea, Forest, Earth, Lavender, Lake and Desert use the
B2T_D3 and B2T_B3 values from lua/base2tone_<name>_dark/palette.lua as
accent and surface seeds respectively. These are seed pairs only;
Material Color Utilities generates both light and dark appearances.
